## Web app

### Image viewer
- See new and every reference you stacked together in the image viewer, each in its tile, zoomed together and compared with new, or with the reference you click to make it the anchor, in grid, wipe, single or difference mode. Untick a reference's tab to leave it out, or pick **One at a time** after the tabs ([several references](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#several-references-at-once)).
- Find which images differ: a panel gives the pixel's values in every image and how far apart each pair is on screen, so zoom into a region to compare it. Click a pair to see it alone. **Images as a contact sheet** shows each run's images in one row ([how far apart](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#how-far-apart), [contact sheet](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#contact-sheet)).
