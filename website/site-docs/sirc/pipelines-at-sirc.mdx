QA-Board now runs [pipelines](/docs/pipelines) itself. HW_ALG had its own pipelines first, in its wrapper for `qa`:
projects with **HW_ALG** in their name keep using the wrapper's pipelines until they opt in. Old branches keep working
as they are, so you can move one branch at a time.

## Try it on one pipeline
Without changing anything in the repository:

```bash
QABOARD_PIPELINES_ENGINE=builtin qa --share batch --runner=local my_pipeline
```

Your pipeline's definition and scripts don't change: `configurations` with `run` and `batch` steps,
`$PIPELINE_OUTPUT_DIR`, *batches.json* and *tuning.json* work the same. You get the steps on the pipeline's card in the
web app, and `{steps.<name>.output_dirs}` in your commands ([use the results of a batch](/docs/pipelines#steps)).

## Move a branch
Add to the product's *qaboard.yaml*:

```yaml title="qaboard.yaml"
pipelines:
  engine: builtin
```

From that commit on, CI runs the pipelines with QA-Board. Branches without it keep the wrapper's.

## What changes
See [what changes when you move to QA-Board's pipelines](/docs/pipelines#before). For HW_ALG:
- Commands with braces that aren't fields, like JSON or `awk '{print $1}'`, now get their other `{fields}` replaced.
- Mistakes in a pipeline's definition stop `qa batch` before anything runs, and a typo in an option close to a known
  one (`use-cahce`) is an error.
- The wrapper's `postprocess()` no longer runs on pipelines, and their metrics no longer have a `step N: ...` entry
  per step.
- `run:` steps with `runner: windows` and batch steps with `commit:` work as before, but QA-Board's tests can't run
  them: check them on your first branch.
